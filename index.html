01  the input

Your numbers, your league

Paste or import the projections and market values you already use. Liquid Sheets scales them into auction dollars for your exact budget, roster, and scoring rules.

02  the promise

Runs offline

Install it like an app and draft with no connection. It was built for the dead ballroom wifi, not undone by it. Every pick is saved on your device as you go.

03  the ethic

Nothing hidden

Every value traces back to an inspectable calculation. The numbers are a model to argue with, not gospel to obey. Open source, MIT licensed, yours to read.
